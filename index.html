Independent, affordable, and rooted in scripture

Lambton Christian School was founded to make a Bible-based education reachable for families in Germiston, without asking them to compromise on academic quality. Every class follows South Africa's CAPS curriculum, so learners build the same foundation as any other school — while growing up in a happy, loving, and caring environment.

We believe character is taught the same way arithmetic is: daily, patiently, and by example. Our staff aim to send learners forward not only prepared for Grade 10, but grounded in who they are.

What guides our classrooms

Three commitments sit behind every lesson, assembly, and report card.

Foundation

Bible-based teaching

Scripture isn't a separate period on the timetable — it shapes how we teach every subject and how staff treat every learner, every day.

Standard

Academic excellence

We follow the CAPS curriculum in full, so learners meet the same national benchmarks as any South African school, taught in smaller classes.

Aim

Godly character

We measure success beyond test scores: honesty, kindness, and responsibility are treated as outcomes we teach toward, not happy accidents.

From Grade RR to Grade 9, one path

Learners move through eleven grades on our campus before continuing to high school — the same core curriculum, taught with continuity from one teacher and grade to the next.
